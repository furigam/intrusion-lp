Introduction

Making ends meet is harder every day. Employers are increaingly demanding speed in the execution of word tasks, competing with robots is not fair - but the game wasn't meant to be fair.

It's just another day at your work and you - the protagonist - recieves another case to solve. But what seemed like the same as usual ends up being something bigger than you could ever imagine, a journey that can challenge your sense of right and wrong.

Team up with fellow investigators at C.I.S.C and uncover the deepest mysteries hidden within society.
